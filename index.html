Tentang kami

Zakat App membantu kakitangan bergaji mendaftar dan mengurus potongan zakat gaji kepada Pusat Zakat Perlis (MAIPs): panduan pendaftaran, penyata zakat tahunan, dan bantuan melalui WhatsApp dan emel.

Zakat App helps salaried employees register and manage their zakat salary deductions to Pusat Zakat Perlis (MAIPs), with step-by-step guides, annual zakat statements, and support by WhatsApp and email.
